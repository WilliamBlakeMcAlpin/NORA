NORA takes the quiet, cult corner.

Loud means the logo or hardware does the talking. Cult means scarcity, numbering and a following the brand does not chase.

JMM is the nearest neighbor, but its collaborations and storytelling pull it toward the center. Maison Margiela is quiet but sells broadly. Nobody else sits where NORA plans to: no exterior mark, a fixed number and one price.

Positions are NORA's judgment, not measured data.
