01 · Positioning

Anti-brand.
Incidentally cool.

NORA looks like it isn't trying to sell anything. The logo is an authentication mark, found inside the temple by the person who owns the pair. Restraint is the brand.

  • Made inSabae, Fukui, Japan. 100%. No offshore or hybrid production.
  • Edition333 per colorway per style. Hand-engraved and numbered, X/333.
  • Price$1,111. One price.
  • StylesBlake, Sunny, Bella.

  • SoldDirect only, at ohitsnora.com. No wholesale.
  • ForEveryone. Unisex from launch.
  • CompanyOhitsnora Corporation (Texas), doing business as NORA Eyewear.
02 · Voice

Near-silent. Facts, not adjectives.

Write like this

Sabae, Japan. 333 pieces. N° 001/333.
Black frame. Black lens.
One price. $1,111.
The waitlist is open.
Hand-engraved inside the temple.

Never like this

Elevate your look with our iconic shades!
We're obsessed with craftsmanship.
Only a few left. Don't miss out!
The ultimate statement piece.
For the modern gentleman.

Short. No exclamation points. No first-person hype. No gendered copy. No invented press, quotes, stockists or numbers.

03 · Banned words

Never in NORA copy.

luxury
exclusive
elevate
premium
curated
iconic
timeless
must-have
game-changer

If a sentence needs one of these words, delete the sentence. The original lockup's "LUXURY EYEWEAR" is kept on file only.

04 · Less is more

Remove until it breaks. Then add one thing back.

Do · one markThe case: a blind deboss of the monogram. Nothing else.
Don't · pattern the logo
Monogram prints, all-over logos, logo hardware.
Do · one line
Sabae, Japan.
One fact per layout.
Don't · explain
Handcrafted with passion by master artisans using only the finest materials, each pair is a testament to our uncompromising commitment to quality and style.
Adjectives, claims, paragraphs.
04 · Less is more

One accent. One line. One mark.

Do · one violet moment
N° —/333
Violet is a spark, never a surface.
Don't · violet text or violet grounds
Body copy in violet on black is hard to read. At 3.4:1 it fails the contrast test for text.
Never as body text, never as a background.
Do · blank exterior The outside of the frame carries nothing.
Don't · more than one line
Boxes, double rules, dividers everywhere.
