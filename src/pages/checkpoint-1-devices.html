The Prism

The leather case is a triangular prism. Its end profile becomes a single hairline triangle: the brand's one shape.

  • Section marker in the guidelines and on the site
  • Instagram story highlight icons (with the monogram)
  • A blind-debossed seal on the box sleeve
  • Never filled, never with the logo inside it

Device 03

The Hairline

The rule that separates monogram and wordmark becomes the structure of every layout. One line, always the same weight, sitting at the golden section. Headlines rest on it; labels hang below it.

  • Instagram feed: the same line height in every post builds a quiet grid
  • Stationery, the authenticity card, the site header
  • One per layout. Never decorative boxes or double rules
